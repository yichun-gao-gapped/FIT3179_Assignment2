How Much of Australia Is Really Protected?

Exploring Australia's terrestrial protected areas using CAPAD 2024.

Protected areas are densely clustered along Australia's east, south-east and south-west coasts, while some of the largest reserves are found across the interior.

Protection is highly uneven across Australia. The ACT and Tasmania protect the largest shares of their land, while Queensland and New South Wales remain close to or below 10%.

Category VI dominates Australia's protected-area network. It contributes about 57.6% of the National Reserve System, more than twice the share of Category II, the second-largest category.
